Pizza

Pizza is a classic Italian dish with flat,round base of dough tha's typically topped with tomato sauce, cheese,and a variety of other ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni

Steps

  1. Roll the dough to make it flat
  2. Spread a layer of tomato sauce over the base
  3. Add mozzerella cheese
  4. Finally bake it in hot over until tyhe crist is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
