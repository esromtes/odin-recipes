Burger

A burger typically consists of a cooked patty of ground meat, often beef,placed inside a sliced bread roll.

Ingredients

  • Beef
  • Sliced burger buns
  • lettuce
  • ripe tomato
  • cheese

Steps

  1. Season the beef with salt and pepper
  2. Cook the beef in hot skillet for 4 minutes
  3. Finally assemble the burger by placing the cooked patty on the bottom bun.
