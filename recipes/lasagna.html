Lasagna

lasagna is a wide,flat pasta dish made by layering sheets of pasta with various ingredients and baked until bubbly.

Ingredients

  • lasagna pasta
  • ground beef
  • Italian sausage
  • tomatoes
  • onions
  • garlic
  • basil
  • ricotta
  • mozzarella

Steps

  1. Cook the beef with onion and garlic until it's browned
  2. Add tomatoes and seasonngs and let it simmer until it's rich and thick
  3. Make a sauce by melting butterm,stirring in flour,and whisking in milk gradually until it thickens.
  4. Assemble everythink in a baking dish by layering by meat sauce,pasta sheets,white sauce,and cheeses.
  5. Finnaly, top with the remaining cheese and bake until golden and bubbly.
